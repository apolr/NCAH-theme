About us

The Association

The Nigerian Canadian Association, Hamilton and Environs (NCAH) is a non-profit organization founded in 1999. We support youth to embrace their heritage, pursue academic success and develop their creativity.

Our Mission

Our mission is to promote unity and provide a conducive environment for individuals with Nigerian heritage to thrive in the community.

Our Goals

  1. Enrich Canadian culture with Nigerian traditions.
  2. Be a welcoming hub for diverse social interaction.
  3. Connect children of Nigerian heritage, and others, to their cultural roots.
  4. Provide essential support to members in need.
  5. Enhance the social and economic well-being of members and the community.
