Membership

What membership means

Join the NCAH community

Membership of the Nigerian Canadian Association, Hamilton and Environs (NCAH) is how you join our community. Members are invited into the NCAH WhatsApp Community, where announcements, events and programs are shared. Registration is voluntary and open to all peoples of Nigeria by birth, naturalization, marriage or heritage, and to others committed to the principles of the Association.

Membership is open to people from other regions and backgrounds who are committed to abiding by the principles and the established goals of the Association. The primary geographic regions that are encompassed in the activities of the Association are the Hamilton-Wentworth region and the surroundings, which include Burlington, Oakville, Kitchener, Waterloo, Guelph, Grimsby and St. Catharines.

How it works

Four simple steps

  1. Complete the form below with your full name, a Canadian WhatsApp number, your address and the name of the person who referred you.
  2. An administrator verifies your address.
  3. Once verified, you receive an invitation to the NCAH WhatsApp Community.
  4. Your registration is valid for one year from the month you register.

Giving is optional

There is no membership fee. If you wish, you can also tick the donor box on the form and tell us how often you would like to give: monthly, quarterly, bi-annually or annually. Donations are voluntary and support our charitable work. They are not required to become a member.
